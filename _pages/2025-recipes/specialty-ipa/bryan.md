# Belgian White Ale

### Fermentables
- 7 lb Pilsner Malt
- 5 lb Wheat Malt
- 2 lb Torrified Wheat
- 5 oz Munich Malt

### Hops
- .6 oz Simcoe at 60 minutes
- .5 oz Cascae at 15 minutes
- .5 oz Amarillo at 5 minutes
- .5 oz Sabro at at 5 minutes
- .5 oz Citra at 0 minutes

### Yeast
- Danstar Abbaey
