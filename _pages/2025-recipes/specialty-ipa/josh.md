# Red Rye IPA

### Fermentables
- 13 lb Marris Otter
- 2.5 lb Rye Malt
- .86 lb Crytal 48.5L
- .639 lb Honey Malt
- .639 lb Carapils
- .419 lb Crystal 81.8L

### Hops
- 1.5 oz Magnum at 60 minutes
- 1 oz Amarillo at 0 minutes
- 1 oz Citra at 0 minutes
- 1 oz Amarillo Dry Hop 3 days
- 1 oz Citra Dry Hop 3 days

### Yeast
- US-05
