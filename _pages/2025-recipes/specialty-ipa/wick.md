# I Rye with my Little Eye

### Fermentables
- 11 lb 2-row
- 3 lb Rye Malt
- 1.25 lb Crystal 55.9L
- .5 lb Wheat Malt
- .2 lb Carafoam

### Hops
- 1 oz Mount Hoood First Wort
- 1.25 oz CTZ at 60 Minutes
- .5 oz Mount Hood at 30 minutes
- 1.5 oz Mount Hood at 0 minutes
- 1 oz CTZ Dry Hop 5 days

### Yeast
- BRY-97
