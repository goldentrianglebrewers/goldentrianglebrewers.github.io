# Cream Ale

### Fermentables
- 4 lb Pale Malt
- 3.5 lb Pilsner Malt
- 1 lb Flaked Corn
- 12 oz Sucrose
- 6.4 oz Acidulated Malt


### Hops
- 0.33 oz Magnum at 60 minutes
- 0.33 oz Magnum at 5 minutes

### Yeast
- CellarScience Cali
