# Palindrome

- OG 1.035
- FG 1.002
- ABV 4.3%

### Fermentables
- 6.5 lb Pilsner Malt
- 2.5 lb Flaked Wheat
- 12 oz Caramel Vienne 20L
- 6 oz Acid Malt

### Hops
- 1 oz Saaz at 10 min
- 1.5 oz Azaaca 15 min hopstand
- 3 oz Azaaca Dry Hop (3 days)

### Yeast
- Bootleg Biology Mad Fermentationis Saison Blend
