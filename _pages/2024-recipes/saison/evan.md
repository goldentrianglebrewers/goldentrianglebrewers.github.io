# Battre L'oie Saison

- OG 1.052
- FG 1.007
- ABV 5.9%

### Fermentables
- 1 lb 5.1 oz Pilsner Malt
- 3.5 oz Table Sugar
- 3.5 oz Vienna Malt
- 3.5 oz Wheat Malt

### Hops
- .2 oz Challenger at 60 minutes
- .2 oz Centinnial at 15 minutes

### Yeast
- Omega Belgian Saison OYL-027
